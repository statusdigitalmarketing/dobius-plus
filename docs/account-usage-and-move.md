# Account usage and Move to account

Three things, all local: the usage panel, Move to account, and the opt-in auto-move on limit that reuses the move.

**Usage panel** (Settings > Accounts > Account usage). For each Claude account: 5-hour and weekly use, pace, a forecast of when it runs dry, and how old the figure is. Figures come from the statusLine reporter (`electron/claude-usage.js`), one record per session under `<userData>/usage`, folded into `<userData>/usage-history.json` by `electron/account-headroom-service.js`. Each window stands on its own, and a window with no live figure reads Unknown, never 0%: real records often carry only `seven_day`, so a row can read 5 hour Unknown, Weekly 99%. A real figure nobody is refreshing is marked stale. A resumed session reports nothing until its first API call on the new account, so a move carries no figures across (checked on real accounts). Records past 8 days are deleted hourly.

**Move to account** (tab context menu). Restarts one tab, or every tab in the window, on another logged-in account and resumes its conversation. The tab is pinned to that account across relaunch, tear-off and reopen. The global active account does not change.

## What a move does

Only one move runs at a time, across all windows. A second one is refused.

**1. Check before touching anything.** Any failure refuses the move with a plain reason and leaves the tab running:

- Exactly one Claude, as a child of the tab's shell. `exec claude`, no Claude, or another program beside it are refused. A Claude that never got a message has no transcript, so there is nothing to resume: it moves as a plain `claude` on the target, verified by account alone.
- Its conversation named by Claude's own `<configDir>/sessions/<pid>.json`, and its working folder readable and present. No auth token and no other HOME or USER in its environment (its login could not be named), and the shell is zsh or bash, which are what can start the resume.
- The target logged in (read last, right before Claude is stopped), and both sides linked to the shared conversation store, not the tab's own credential and not the same login. A login that cannot be named is refused, on either side: another spelling of a folder (`/p/b/.`, `/p/b/`, a symlink), a credential or metadata file linked out of its folder, a hard-linked credential file, or two default metadata files that disagree.
- No input since the tab was judged. Move all also skips tabs mid-turn, on a question, or with unsent text, and names them. A single-tab move names a stopped turn or lost text in its confirm, read at the click, and main refuses a draft that appeared after that.
- A tab on Claude's limit menu is neither mid-turn nor a draft, since that is the main reason to move. It takes all of: the menu's own rows as the last rows on screen (top edge, question, numbered options led by `1. Stop and wait for limit to reset`, one pointer, the `Enter to confirm` footer), Claude's record saying `waiting`, and a hook status other than `needs`. On real 2.1.316 the record says `waiting` and the hook goes `working` then `idle`, never `needs` over 150s. Any other drawing falls back to busy, the safe way.

**2. Stop and restart.** SIGHUP to Claude, a bounded wait for it to exit, a claim on the conversation, then a new login shell on the target with the resume started by the launch path (`loginShellSpawn` in `shared/new-tab.js`). Nothing is typed, so keys pressed meanwhile cannot mix into a command, and a slow shell rc only uses up the wait. The claim is released on every abort after it.

**3. Verify.** Success is reported only when the new process's own environment (`claude-process-scan.js`) names the target's credential, carries no auth token, its own `sessions/<pid>.json` names the conversation, and the target folder still names the same login. Otherwise the dialog says what is really running, and the pin follows that rather than the request. Two endings do not wait out the 45 seconds: the target asking whether to trust this folder (answer it in the tab, the conversation carries on), and Claude exiting right after it started.

IPC, all owner-checked per tab: `accounts:usage`, `accounts:moveTargets`, `accounts:moveCandidates`, `accounts:move`.

## Auto-move on limit (opt-in)

Settings > Accounts > Account usage > "Move a tab to another account when it hits its limit". Off by default, and inactive while the usage reporter is off or the Switch job is re-logging folders; the Switch job in turn refuses to start while any move is running, or for 10 minutes (as long as the conversation stays claimed) after a move whose resumed conversation was not confirmed running (a startup file may run some other `claude` first). Every 30 seconds main (`autoTick` in `electron/account-headroom-service.js`) looks at each tab a window owns:

- **A limit** is exactly the "limit menu" test above (the menu's rows at the live bottom of the screen, Claude's record `waiting`, hook not `needs`). Only a tab whose record says `waiting` gets the full look. A 100% figure alone never moves anything.
- **A target** must pass the dialog's own refusal (logged in, a different login, shared history) and, on top: a known five-hour figure (a weekly-only record is not enough), not at its limit, at least 20% left in every window after 15 points per automatic attempt into it in the last 10 minutes (a failed one too: its resume may still be waiting on a slow shell; charged to the login picked and the one the move's own read found), no window forecast to run out within 30 minutes, and its login not seen on its limit menu since its five-hour window last reset (when no reset time is known, for five hours). Every tab is looked at before any moves, so a login shown on its limit in one tab is never a target for another, whichever comes first. A process list that could not list every Claude, any tab whose Claude is not in the process list (read again once), a tab whose shell became Claude (`exec claude`, or a pid the process list could not resolve), a tab whose Claude was replaced while it was looked at, a tab whose Claude's own record could not be read, a tab whose shell runs Claude next to another program, a tab waiting on a dialog whose screen could not be read, and a tab on its limit menu whose login cannot be named (no address, or a token in its environment deciding it), stops every move that tick, since a limit there cannot be held. Unknown is never a target. The most room wins. Cooldowns, holds and reservations are kept in `<userData>/auto-move.json`, saved before Claude is stopped (an attempt that cannot be saved is not tried, and a file that exists but cannot be read stops auto-move until it can), so a relaunch (which restores the same tab ids) does not reset them. A tab with input in the last 30 seconds waits for the next tick, with no cooldown.
- **The move** is `moveOne`, the dialog's move, at most one attempt per tick (a target can hit its own limit as the moved tab resumes, and only the next look sees it), under the same one-at-a-time lock, refusing a busy tab or unsent text. Right before Claude is stopped it checks again, on the move's own fresh reads: the tab is still on its limit menu, nothing was typed since the tick looked, it is the same Claude the tick judged, the process list is complete, its Claude runs on the folder its tab was started with (otherwise a shell setting chooses the folder and would choose it for the restart too), and the target still qualifies. A tick that finds a manual move running stands aside and tries next time, with no cooldown. The resumed Claude starts as `claude --resume <id> '<carry-on prompt>'` (`CARRY_ON_PROMPT`, `shared/new-tab.js`): the prompt is on the command line, never typed into the tab. The new shell sends it only when, after its startup files, precmd hooks and the `cd` into the conversation's folder (directory hooks) ran, the `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` it would hand Claude (exported, read with printenv) is exactly the folder that passed the checks and none of the token variables that decide a login instead (`CLAUDE_AUTH_ENV`) is exported with a value (tested by exit status, so `set -x` in a startup file never prints a key); otherwise it resumes without it, and verification reports what runs. Main also drops the carry-on when the spawn's folder is not the checked one (an account edited meanwhile).
- **Cooldown.** Any attempt, moved or failed, leaves that tab alone for 15 minutes, also across its window closing and the project reopening it. With nowhere to go it stays and is looked at again next time.
- **What the window is told** (`accounts:autoMove`, `src/components/Project/AutoMoveNotices.jsx`): which tab, from which account to which and why, or why it stayed, once per limit. The pin is applied exactly as after a manual move.

**Claude in Chrome.** Read from the code of Claude Code 2.1.319 and extension 1.0.105 (not tried live on two accounts): Claude Code reaches the extension through `wss://bridge.claudeusercontent.com/chrome/<account uuid>` with its own OAuth token, the extension connects to the same bridge with its OWN token from its own claude.ai sign-in, and Claude Code refuses a bridge socket for another account ("Closing the bridge socket: it connected as a different account"; its tool error asks for "the same claude.ai account as Claude Code"). So a tab on another account cannot use the extension, however it got there. A move never changes the extension: only an account already logged in is a target, nothing runs `claude auth login`, and no browser opens. What does touch it is a browser sign-in (the Switch job's `auth login`, or `/login` in a tab): it opens claude.ai in Chrome, and signing in there as another account signs Chrome's claude.ai into that account, which the extension follows the next time it signs in. The notice on a moved tab says browser tools there work only if the extension is on that account.

## Known limits

Accepted, not fixed. Each is a wrong or missing figure, or a refusal that does not fire, in the situation described.

1. **First sight of a folder.** With no history yet for a folder, a session still running on it can repaint an earlier login's cached figure after the boundary, and that figure shows as the current login's until a new one arrives.
2. **Out-of-order record.** A record that only becomes readable later, holding a higher figure with an older time, shows with the age of the newer lower report.
3. **Token inherited at launch.** With `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN` in Dobius's own environment, moves are refused, but usage from a session in folder B is still recorded against B's email although it is the token's login.
4. **Home-level metadata symlink.** `~/.claude.json` linked to another profile's file names the wrong login for Default, so a same-login move can be allowed. A metadata or credential file linked inside a profile folder is caught; this home-level one is not.
5. **Unlisted alias login.** Logging in through a spelling not listed in Accounts (`CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=/p/b/. claude`) rewrites the folder's metadata while the listed spelling's Keychain item stays on the old login, so a same-login move can be allowed. Step 3 cannot see this either, because the credential key matches.
6. **Two profiles on one login.** Re-logging one of them clears that login's higher figure, so the other row can show a lower one until its next report.
7. **Report right after a login.** A valid report that arrives just before the next sample is taken for the old login's and ignored until its figure changes. The row stays Unknown meanwhile, which is the safe direction.
8. **Placeholder drawn without dim.** Claude's placeholder and ghost suggestion are recognised by the dim attribute, confirmed on a 2.1.314 frame. A version that draws them otherwise reads as unsent text, so Move all skips that tab and names it.
9. **Token no process shows.** A token set inside Claude's own settings files (an `env` block, `apiKeyHelper`) is applied inside Claude and never appears in its process environment, so step 3 cannot see it and reports success. A token exported by the shell is seen and reported, by name only.

10. **Relaunch folder after a subfolder resume.** Auto-resume restores a moved conversation with the right id and account, but in the project folder rather than the subfolder it was resumed from: it does this for any conversation resumed from a subfolder, moved or not.
11. **A subfolder conversation with no link yet.** Moved before Dobius has linked it (the first 15 seconds or so), its link names the subfolder, so auto-resume cannot find the transcript after a relaunch and skips it. Nothing is lost: it can be resumed by hand from History.

12. **Move all right after a Claude starts.** For its first several seconds a new Claude reads as working, so Move all leaves it where it is and names it under "Left where they are". Run Move all again and it moves. Treating a never-messaged Claude as movable was tried and dropped: a first message can be in flight with no sign of it yet (just typed, or passed on the command line), and moving then would lose it.

13. **Auto-move into a limit the figures do not show.** A login seen on its limit menu is held until its five-hour window resets, or for five hours when no reset time is known. A weekly limit that outlasts that, with figures that never reach 100%, lets one tab move into it after the hold, stop on the limit menu again, sit out its 15-minute cooldown and move on. A limit refusal spends no usage.
14. **Auto-move's carry-on and a `claude` wrapper.** The shell sends the carry-on only when its own `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` is the target's and no login token is set, but a shell function or alias named `claude` that sets another folder or a token only for its own command, or a startup file that changes HOME or USER, is not seen, and the prompt goes out on that login. The move then reports the wrong account and the tab keeps it, as a manual move would.
15. **Browser tools in a moved tab.** Claude in Chrome follows the one account its extension is signed in to (above), so they stop working in a tab moved to another account. This cannot be fixed from Dobius.
16. **A resume queued past the claim, now with a prompt.** The open item below (a target shell blocked over 10 minutes while the same conversation is resumed elsewhere) applies to an automatic move too, and its queued resume also carries the carry-on prompt into the second Claude.
17. **A pin lost with its window.** The pin reaches the window that owned the tab when the move ended. A tab torn off or a window closed in between keeps running on the new account but forgets the pin on relaunch, as after a manual move.
18. **Auto-move trusts a figure until its window resets.** A target's last five-hour report counts until its reset time, even when no session on this Mac has refreshed it for hours. Another Mac using that login meanwhile can leave it with less than the report says, and the moved tab can land on its limit menu again (no usage is spent; the login is then held). Refusing figures nobody refreshed would also refuse the usual target, an idle spare account, whose last report is old by nature.
19. **A target re-logged during the move's seconds.** A `claude auth login` run outside Dobius on the target's folder between the move's last check and the new shell starting Claude (a few seconds) sends the carry-on on the new login; verification then reports it. The Switch job is excluded: neither starts while the other runs, and the Switch job also waits out a move whose Claude had not started yet.
20. **Claude in a nested shell.** A tab running `bash` (or another shell) inside its own shell, with Claude under that, is not looked at: the lookup sees only the tab shell's direct children. If that Claude sits on its limit menu, its login is not held, and another tab can move into it once (then it is held from that tab's own limit menu). Manual moves refuse such a tab as well.
21. **A Claude lookup that times out for one tab.** The per-tab child lookup (`pgrep`) failing while the process scan succeeds makes that tab look like a plain shell for that tick. If its Claude sits on its limit menu, its login is not held that tick (Codex round 8, High). Treating every tab with some other program in front as unknown would stop auto-move for anyone with a dev server or editor open.
22. **Turning auto-move off does not cancel a move already started.** The carry-on is on the new shell's command line; switching the setting off while that shell is still starting does not withdraw it (Codex round 8, Medium).
23. **Two copies of Dobius on one userData.** Each keeps its own copy of the auto-move memory and the last to save wins, so one can drop a cooldown the other saved (Codex round 10). config.json has the same last-writer rule; the single-instance lock normally prevents this.

## Open, for Sam to judge

Found by Codex on the final pass, not fixed, not accepted.

- **A resume queued for more than 10 minutes (rated High by all three lenses).** The hold on a conversation expires after 10 minutes. If the target's shell startup is blocked that long, and the same conversation is resumed in another tab meanwhile, the queued resume also starts when the shell finishes, and two Claudes write one transcript. Needs a shell blocked over 10 minutes and a manual resume of that conversation.
- **Cleanup racing a swapped folder.** Replacing the usage folder with a symlink between the check and the listing could delete a file named like a session record in the other folder. Needs local write access, and a file with a session-id name.
- **A shell wrapper that changes the resume** keeps the hold after the mismatch, so that conversation can't be moved for up to 10 minutes.
- **A phone replacing a draft during the confirm** can lose the new text.
- **`USER='sam TAG=x'`** passes the environment check as `sam`. Needs a crafted Keychain item.
